Observations
What is already known that bears on the goal. The agent searches Amass and summarises each record; drop the ones that do not belong and correct the summaries, since the builder is shown this list as it chooses its nodes and thresholds. A summary is the agent's reading of the record, not a verified claim. Left empty, the builder searches for itself as it builds.
